An infographic can be defined as a visual representation of 
information and data (Oxford English Dictionary). Specifically, 
an infographic is a combination of graphics, data visualizations 
such as pie charts and bar graphs, and minimal text that provides 
a simple overview of a subject. An infographic, which combines 
text, image, chart, diagram, and video, is an excellent tool for
